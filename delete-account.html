TukDrive Account Deletion

Last Updated: September 2026

Users may request deletion of their TukDrive account and associated personal data.

To request account deletion, please contact:

support@tukdrive.com

Please include:

  • Your registered phone number
  • Your registered email address (if available)
  • Your account type (Customer or Driver)

After verification, the account deletion request will be processed.

Certain records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory purposes.
